# Kharis Church App

## Product Purpose
Mobile app for Kharis Church members and visitors. Surfaces sermons (audio from SoundCloud, video from YouTube), events, giving, daily reading, and prayer across all church branches.

## Users
- **Members**: regular attendees across UK branches (London, Birmingham, Reading, Chatham, Croydon, etc.) and international (Accra, Freetown). Consume sermons weekly, give regularly, attend events.
- **New Here**: first-time visitors exploring the church. Need branch discovery, service times, and a low-friction introduction.
- **Guests**: casual listeners browsing the sermon library without church membership.

## Brand
- **Name**: Kharis (Greek: grace/favour)
- **Tagline**: Changing the world with a touch of His grace
- **Senior Pastor**: David Antwi
- **Tone**: welcoming, warm, grounded, not flashy. Premium but accessible. A church that takes its craft seriously without being performative about it.
- **Logo**: Dove mark in purple (#6B34FA)

## Anti-references
- The old Kharis app: white backgrounds mixed with dark headers, YouTube embed with red play button, bank details in plaintext, "Calender" typo, off-brand teal, baked-in text on thumbnails, flat cards with no hierarchy.
- Generic church apps with stock imagery and default system fonts.
- Spotify clones that copy the layout but miss the warmth a church needs.

## Strategic Principles
1. The app serves the church, not the other way around. Content (sermons, prayer, scripture) is the product. UI gets out of the way.
2. Every component must work standalone before it composes. No component exists only as part of a page.
3. Colours and button styles come from kharis.org exactly. Design patterns come from studied Mobbin references.
4. Dark mode first. The old app's white backgrounds are the anti-pattern.
5. Audio is the hero feature. The player (mini and full) must be as good as Spotify's, not an afterthought.
