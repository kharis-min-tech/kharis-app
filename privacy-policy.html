Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 2026  ·  Kharis Ministries

This policy explains how the Kharis Church mobile application collects, uses, and protects your personal information. By using the app, you agree to the practices described below.

Who We Are

The Kharis Church app is developed and maintained by Kharis Ministries. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data with transparency and care.

For any privacy-related questions, contact us at info@kharis.org.

Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide you with a personalised church app experience.

Account & Profile
  • Email address — used for account creation and authentication
  • Display name — shown within the app
  • Selected branch — chosen manually by you from a list
  • Role preference — e.g. member, visitor (selected by you)
App Usage Data
  • Playback history for sermons and audio content (stored locally on your device)
  • Resume positions for media content (stored locally on your device)
  • Push notification preferences
  • Device push token — used by Firebase to deliver notifications to your device

Information We Do Not Collect

We want to be explicit about what we do not collect or have access to:

Payment information Location data Contacts Photos or camera Microphone Other device data

Giving and tithes are handled entirely through an external website operated by a third party. We never see, store, or process any payment card or banking information within the app.

Branch selection is done manually by you; the app never requests or accesses your device location.

How We Use Your Information

  1. To create and manage your account and authenticate you securely
  2. To personalise your experience based on your chosen branch and role
  3. To remember your media playback positions across sessions
  4. To send relevant push notifications (only if you grant permission)
  5. To improve app functionality and resolve technical issues

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Third-Party Services

The app integrates the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

Firebase (Google)
  • Authentication — secure sign-in and account management
  • Firestore / Cloud Storage — account data and app content
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) — push notifications via device token
SoundCloud
  • Streams sermon and worship audio content embedded within the app
  • SoundCloud's own privacy policy governs data collected during playback
YouTube
  • Delivers video content (services, events, worship) embedded within the app
  • YouTube / Google's privacy policy governs data collected during playback
Google Fonts
  • Provides typography via the Google Fonts CDN; standard Google Fonts usage applies

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of these services.

Data Storage & Security

Your account data is stored securely on Firebase servers, which run on Google Cloud infrastructure. Google maintains industry-standard physical, administrative, and technical safeguards.

Playback history and resume positions are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to our servers.

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no system is completely secure. We encourage you to use a strong, unique password for your account.

Data Retention

Your account data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you request deletion of your account, we will remove your personal information from our systems within a reasonable timeframe, except where retention is required by law.

Push notification tokens are removed when you delete your account or revoke notification permissions on your device.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete your account and associated personal data
  • Withdraw consent for push notifications at any time via your device settings
  • Object to or restrict certain processing of your data

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@kharis.org.

Children's Privacy

The Kharis Church app is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately at info@kharis.org and we will take prompt steps to delete such information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make significant changes, we will notify you through the app. The effective date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent revision.

Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal data, please reach out:

Kharis Ministries — Privacy info@kharis.org

We aim to respond to all privacy enquiries within 5 business days.
