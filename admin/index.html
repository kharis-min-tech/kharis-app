Publish Announcement

An announcement is a message to the church: headline, body, optional image, optional expiry. It shows in the announcements carousel on the app home screen. Anything with a date and a venue that people RSVP to belongs in the Events tab.

Leave the time blank to publish now when the date is today, or at 09:00 UK time on a later date.

Published Announcements

A member sees an announcement on the app home screen when it is set to their branch or to All campuses, its publish time has arrived, and it has not expired. Each row says exactly where it stands.
