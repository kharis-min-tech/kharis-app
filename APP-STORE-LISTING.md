### Description (3,847 chars)
```
Kharis Church is the official app for Kharis Ministries -- a family of churches across the United Kingdom and West Africa, led by Senior Pastor David Antwi.

Whether you are a long-standing member or someone who just walked through the doors for the first time, this app is built to keep you connected to the life of the church no matter where you are.

SERMONS

Every message preached at Kharis is available to you here. Listen to audio or watch video recordings of Sunday services and midweek teachings. Sermons are organised by series so you can work through a topic from start to finish. The built-in player keeps your place, so you can pause on your commute and pick up later at home.

DAILY PRAYER

Each day you will find a fresh prayer rooted in Scripture. It is something short and grounding -- a moment to centre yourself before the day gets going. Tap Pray With Us to add your voice to what the church is believing together.

EVENTS

Stay up to date with what is happening across the Kharis family. From Sunday services and prayer nights to special gatherings and outreach events, the calendar shows you what is coming up at your branch and beyond. You can save events directly to your phone calendar so nothing slips through.

GIVING

Supporting the work of the ministry is straightforward through the app. Tap Give and you will be taken securely to the Kharis giving page where you can make a one-time gift or set up regular giving. Your generosity goes directly toward services, community programmes, and mission work.

YOUR BRANCH

Kharis Ministries has congregations in London, Birmingham, Reading, Chatham, Croydon, and Medway in the UK, as well as in Accra, Ghana and Freetown, Sierra Leone. When you first open the app, you choose your home branch. Everything from service times to local events is tailored to where you belong, while you still have access to content from across the whole ministry.

NOTIFICATIONS

Turn on notifications and you will hear from us when a new sermon is published, when an event is coming up, or when there is something important the church wants to share. You are in control of what you receive and when.

BUILT FOR EVERYONE

The app is designed to be simple. You do not need to create an account to listen to sermons or read the daily prayer -- just open it and it is there. If you do sign in, you can save your branch preference and keep your giving history in one place.

Kharis means grace. That word shapes everything we do -- how we preach, how we serve, and how we welcome people. We hope this app feels like an extension of that.

kharis.org
```

### What's New -- v2.0
```
Version 2.0 is a complete rebuild of the Kharis app from the ground up.

The new app is faster and more reliable. Sermon playback now works properly in the background, so you can listen while your phone is locked or while using other apps. The daily prayer card is new and refreshes every day with Scripture.

We rebuilt the giving flow so it loads more quickly and works consistently across all devices. The events calendar is now connected live to the church's schedule, so you will always see what is actually happening rather than out-of-date information.

The design has been overhauled -- cleaner, easier to read, and more comfortable to use in low light. Branch selection now happens at the start so the content you see is relevant to you from the moment you open the app.

If you ran into problems with the old app, we think you will find this version much better. Thank you for your patience while we got it right.
```
